3. Permissions

Our Games may request the following device permissions to ensure core functionality and enhance your gaming experience. These permissions are strictly necessary for the features described below:

  • Vibration Access: This permission enables in-game haptic feedback to improve the gaming experience.

  • Internet Access: Access to network interfaces enables:

    • Downloading essential game content updates;

    • Transmitting crash reports and analytics events;

    • Loading advertising content.

    • Disabling this permission will restrict online functionality.

  • Network State Access: This permission permits the Games to detect active internet connections and connection types (e.g., Wi-Fi or mobile data). Its purpose is to:

    • Disable network-dependent features when offline;

    • Optimize bandwidth usage;

    • Prevent unnecessary data consumption.

  • Wake Lock Access: Permission to prevent device sleep mode is required to maintain screen illumination during uninterrupted gameplay sessions.

  • Bluetooth Peripheral Connectivity: Where hardware support exists, Bluetooth access may be requested to:

    • Establish connections with external controllers;

    • Pair with audio output devices.

    • We expressly declare that:

      • No Bluetooth scanning occurs without user initiation;

      • Only paired device names are processed;

      • MAC addresses or other persistent identifiers are neither collected nor stored.

  • Advertising Identifier Access: Access to the operating system's resettable advertising identifier (AAID on Android, IDFA on iOS) is utilized for:

    • Personalizing advertising content via Google AdMob;

    • Analytical measurement through Firebase.

    • Users may reset this identifier or opt-out of personalized advertising through platform settings:

      • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID / Opt-out

      • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

  • Install Attribution Measurement: Through Google Play Install Referrer API, non-personal referrer parameters are collected exclusively for campaign attribution analysis. This data is processed by Firebase and cannot identify individual users.

We only request the necessary permissions for the app's functionality. Importantly, we do not share any personal data with third parties, and we do not collect any personal data directly without your consent. The data collected by third-party services is governed by their privacy policies, as mentioned above.

Our native mobile applications (Games) do not implement first-party cookies. However, embedded third-party SDKs (e.g., Firebase, AdMob) in these applications may utilize:

  • Device identifiers for ad frequency capping;

  • Tracking pixels for conversion measurement;

  • SDK-specific tokens for analytics session continuity.
